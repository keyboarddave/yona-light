Low-battery steps only act on battery power, after the level has been past the line for 10 s. In the "off" band the light won't turn back on until it's charged.

Button

Holding the button passes two marks: a tick at 1 s (let go now for the long-press action), then the hold action fires at 3 s. Factory default: long = Find, hold = off, so off is the deliberate one. With the light off, any press except one mapped to Find turns it on. Setting double press to "Nothing" makes a single press act at once (no 0.35 s wait).

Recorder

Logs every status message (every 2 s, over USB or Bluetooth) for charge, discharge and soak tests. Keep this page open; it carries on across a Bluetooth reconnect.

Nothing recorded.

Battery calibration

Best on battery (USB unplugged). Meter the battery + rail to GND, type the reading and add it. One point fixes the gain; a second point at least 0.15 V away (e.g. low, then full) fixes the offset too. Saved on the light; factory reset keeps it.

USB only; nothing is saved and a restart clears it. Runs the low-battery steps as if on battery at that level (they act after 10 s).

Bluetooth pairing

A phone or computer pairs once by typing the light's 6-digit passkey, then reconnects without asking. New pairings only work for 3 minutes after the light starts or its button is pressed, and whenever it's on USB, so nobody can pair while Yona is out. The passkey is shown here over USB only.

Animate memory chips

Each slot chip plays its saved look, like the Output ring. Off shows still pictures. This is a setting of this browser, not the light.

Reset

Puts every setting and all the memory slots back to the factory defaults. Battery calibration and the passkey are kept. Over USB it also forgets paired Bluetooth devices; over Bluetooth it doesn't, so the phone doing it isn't locked out.

Serial log

Writes every line in and out, with the time and the link, to a text file you choose (e.g. a logs folder in the project), as it happens. Chrome on a computer only.
