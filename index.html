Low-battery steps only act on battery power, after the level has been past the line for 10 s. In the "off" band the light won't turn back on until it's charged.

Button

Holding the button passes two marks: a tick at 1 s (let go now for the long-press action), then the hold action fires at 3 s. Factory default: long = Find, hold = off, so off is the deliberate one. With the light off, any press except one mapped to Find turns it on. Setting double press to "Nothing" makes a single press act at once (no 0.35 s wait).
